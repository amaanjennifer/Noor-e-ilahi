Noor-e-Ilahi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

Reading the Quran, Hadith, Duas, Tasbeeh, Qibla and prayer times needs no account. Settings, bookmarks and tasbeeh counts are stored only on your device.

If you join the community (Ummah), we store your email address, username, password (securely hashed by our provider), and the posts, likes, follows, chat messages, reports and blocks you create.

Location

If you tap “Use my location”, your approximate coordinates are used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. They are sent to the prayer-time and map-name services needed to do this and are saved only on your device.

Third-party services

Supabase (accounts and community data), Al Quran Cloud (Quran text and audio), Aladhan (prayer times), Open-Meteo (city search), hadith-api (Hadith text).

Safety

Community posts and messages can be reported or blocked. Content that breaks the rules may be removed and accounts may be suspended.

Your choices

You can log out at any time. To delete your account and data, contact us using the details below.
